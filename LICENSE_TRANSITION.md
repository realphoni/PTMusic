# PTMusic license transition

Starting with PTMusic 26.9.3, Phoni Technology is moving away from the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 to its own End-User License Agreement (EULA): the Phoni Technology Semi-Free Source License (PTSFSL).

The current terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). They permit personal, non-commercial use and require credit for publicly shared forks. Modifications are permitted only when based on source from an official main release whose version ends in .0 (the year.month.0 form) and which was released under PTSFSL. A patch release such as 26.9.3 may be used or forked without changes, but not modified under these terms.

This transition is not retroactive. Releases before 26.9.3 continue under the license distributed with those releases. Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.
